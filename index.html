WE DON'T TRY TO DO EVERYTHING. WE FOCUS ON ONE THING.

FORMA Focuses Exclusively on Packaging — from the First Idea to Production-Ready Design.

Logos, banners, websites, and social media matter — but they are not our specialization. FORMA focuses on packaging design from the first concept through production preparation. That focus lets us go deeper into how a product looks, is perceived, and performs in the real world.

1

Understand the Product

We study the product itself, its audience, price segment, competitors, and sales environment. For us, packaging is not just attractive graphics — it is part of the product and its positioning.

2

Design the Packaging, Not Just the Image

We account for structure, format, materials, dimensions, element placement, and the realities of printing. The design is created with an understanding of how it will look not only on screen, but in the customer's hands.

3

Build a System, Not Just One Beautiful Pack

We build a visual system that can expand across new flavors, formats, and SKUs. The range stays coherent and recognizable as it grows.

4

Take the Design All the Way to Production

We check technical requirements, CMYK, bleed, fonts, images, and other print-critical parameters. You receive more than a concept — you receive artwork ready to move into production.
